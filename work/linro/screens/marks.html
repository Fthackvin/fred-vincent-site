Interaction marks

Bauhaus blocks cut from the wordmark: same corner, same 28° terminal, in whatever size and proportion the composition needs. White is a thing that is present and recorded. An outline is the same thing gone quiet. A ghost with moving lines is something under suspicion. Red is an alert, violet is drift, blue is a policy boundary. Every animation acts out the process it names.
