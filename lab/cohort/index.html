Reconstruction of the cohort.art project, featuring In Absentia 2021/22 · Back to Lab

Contemporary British Painting Prize 2021

View show

The Contemporary British Painting Prize 2021 brought together work selected by David Ainley, Karl Bielik, Sikelela Owen and Joanna Whittle. Susan Absolon received the prize; Hannah Murgatroyd was highly commended. Cohort hosted the online presentation of the sixty longlisted painters.

This partial reconstruction presents one verified painting from each of fourteen artists on the wall. All sixty longlisted artist profiles and forty-five works by the fifteen shortlisted artists are available in the recovered catalogue. The remaining longlist submissions and original wall positions have not been recovered.

Archive and sources
